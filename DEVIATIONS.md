Pre-data, 1st of June 2026. Marker-to-sample alignment changed from constant wall_clock_anchor offset to per-recording linear regression of counter-derived acquisition time on wall-clock receive times. Motivation: pilot diagnostics revealed ~1.2-1.4 ms/s drift between host clock and Unicorn oscillator, accumulating to several hundred ms over multi-minute recordings and pushing ERP windows out of expected latencies. The new method absorbs both Bluetooth pipeline delay and per-session clock drift as a data-driven correction with no hardcoded constants. Change made before any subject data was collected. Method described in detail in analysis/loader.py.
